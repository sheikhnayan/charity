🔧 Database Schema Requirements

Sponsor Model Fields: - id (primary key) - website_id (foreign key to websites table) - image (path to sponsor logo) - created_at, updated_at (timestamps) Relationships: - Sponsor belongs to Website - Website has many Sponsors
